How it works

Four steps, applied to any process

The same loop runs an invoice, a claim, a service ticket or a maintenance alert. You decide where a person must approve.

  1. 1

    Read

    Documents, emails, screens and sensor data are extracted with confidence scores, including scans, handwriting and multiple languages.

  2. 2

    Reason

    The agent applies your policies, thresholds and approval rules. If a case is unclear, it routes it to a person instead of guessing.

  3. 3

    Act

    It posts, updates and notifies in SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow and Microsoft 365 through validated connectors, with screen automation only as a fallback.

  4. 4

    Prove

    Inputs, outputs, actions and approvals are recorded in a replayable audit trail your finance and risk teams can inspect.

Questions we hear

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the RPA we already have?

RPA replays recorded steps and stops when anything deviates. A Volens agent understands the goal and your policies, so it handles variation, explains what it did, and asks a person when it should. Existing bots can be migrated gradually; nothing has to be replaced on day one.

Will an agent take actions we have not approved?

No. Each agent runs inside a policy you define: which systems it may use, which thresholds need approval, and which actions are never automated. The runtime enforces those limits, and every action is on the record.

Where does our data go?

You choose: Volens Cloud in India or the EU, your own AWS, Google Cloud or Azure account, or fully inside your network. We never train models on customer data, and encryption keys can be customer-managed.

Which systems can you connect to?

SAP, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft 365, Dynamics 365, Workday and Oracle through typed connectors, any REST or OData API quickly, and legacy desktop or web applications through computer-use agents when there is no API.

How long until something is live?

A scoped pilot runs on live volume within six to eight weeks of kick-off, after a two-week discovery. The target is a signed-off production agent inside about ninety days.

Do we need our own AI team?

Not to start. Our engineers build the first agents with you and hand over with runbooks and training. Many customers then grow an internal team on top of the platform; we support either path.

What does it cost?

Discovery is fixed-price, and the pilot is quoted at the end of discovery based on volume and systems involved. Design partners receive preferential pricing in return for feedback and reference rights. Request a call and we will give you a range for your process.
